# Product Vision

## Positioning

TravelOS is a connected operating system for the travel lifecycle: discover, plan, coordinate, manage, and eventually book travel.

## Initial users

- Individual travellers, couples, families, and groups.
- Later: travel advisors, agencies, tour operators, and verified travel providers.

## Traveller-first MVP

1. Account and profile management.
2. Create and manage trips.
3. AI-assisted itinerary drafts with explicit user approval.
4. Map-based itinerary exploration.
5. Multi-currency budget planning.
6. Group invitations and collaboration.
7. Expense splitting and settlement suggestions.

## Product principles

- Global by design: destinations are not restricted to a single country.
- Trust before automation: never fabricate availability, prices, bookings, or verification.
- Human approval: AI suggestions do not silently change a user's trip.
- Clear provenance: distinguish user-entered, AI-estimated, and supplier-confirmed information.
- Privacy and security: least-privilege access and explicit consent for consequential actions.

## Differentiation hypotheses to validate

- A trip-aware assistant that supports travellers throughout the journey, not only at planning time.
- Cross-supplier trip context and disruption support.
- Better group decision-making across preferences and budgets.
- Transparent, evidence-backed recommendations.

These are hypotheses, not proven market gaps. Validate them through interviews and competitor testing.
